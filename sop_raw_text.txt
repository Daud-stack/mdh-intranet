P: 1. Policy Statement
P: Mbuya Dorcas Hospital is committed to ensuring that all cash transactions are handled securely, accurately, and transparently. This policy establishes the required controls to prevent fraud, misappropriation, and financial discrepancies.
P: 2. Purpose
P: To define the standard procedures for cash collection, receipting, safeguarding, and daily reconciliation in all revenue‑generating departments.
P: 3. Scope
P: This SOP applies to:
P: This SOP applies to:
P: Cashiers
P: Accounts Department
P: Front Office Staff
P: Departmental Managers
P: Finance Manager
P: 4. Definitions
P: Cash: Physical currency (notes and coins) received from patients.
P: Float: Pre‑approved amount issued to cashiers at the start of shift.
P: Reconciliation: Verification that cash received matches system‑generated reports.
P: Receipt: Official hospital document acknowledging payment.
P: 5. Roles & Responsibilities
T: [['Role', 'Responsibility'], ['Front Office Supervisor', 'Verify receipts, monitor cashier performance'], ['Cashier Collect\nAccounts Officer', 'payments, issue receipts, safeguard cash\nPerform daily reconciliation and banking'], ['Finance Manager', 'Approve discrepancies, oversee compliance']]
P: 6. Procedure
P: A. Start‑of‑Day Cash Float
P: Cashier receives authorised float from Accounts.
P: Both parties sign the Float Issue Register.
P: Cashier verifies amount before accepting.
P: B. Cash Collection
P: Cashier enters payment into the billing system.
P: Issue an official hospital receipt immediately.
P: Provide the patient with:
P: Original receipt
P: Change (if applicable)
P: No transaction may be processed without a receipt.
P: C. Cash Security
P: Cash must be kept in a locked cash drawer at all times.
P: Cashiers must not mix personal money with hospital funds.
P: Cash drawer access is restricted to the assigned cashier only.
P: D. End‑of‑Day Reconciliation
P: Cashier prints:
P: Daily Collection Report
P: Receipt Summary
P: Count cash physically.
P: Compare physical cash with system totals.
P: Record findings in the Daily Cash Reconciliation Sheet.
P: E. Submission to Accounts
P: Cashier submits:
P: Cash
P: Reconciliation Sheet
P: System reports
P: Accounts Officer verifies and signs off.
P: Any discrepancies must be reported immediately.
P: F. Banking
P: Accounts Officer prepares banking deposit.
P: Finance Manager reviews and approves deposit slip.
P: Funds are banked daily.
P: 7. Documentation & Records
P: Float Issue Register
P: Daily Cash Reconciliation Sheet
P: Receipt Summary Report
P: Banking Deposit Slip
P: Cash Discrepancy Report (if applicable)
P: 8. Compliance Requirements
P: 100% of receipts must be system‑generated.
P: Cash must be banked daily without exception.
P: All discrepancies must be investigated within 24 hours.
P: Surprise audits may be conducted by Finance Manager.
